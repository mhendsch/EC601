## Overall Log
Both AIs, without looking at each other's responses, agreed that there was a great risk that our project would learn a person's baseline rather than a general relationship. Both warned that if someone appeared in multiple datasets, our project might overfit to that person. This would make it extremely accurate for that particular person, but then it would not be generalizable. Both AIs also warned that our baselines were "jumping the gun," meaning we rushed to put numbers (like within 10%) on things before we even achieved correlation. They also criticized our use of 10% without anything more specific (such as 5k time). Where the two differed was in their review of our kill criterion. Claude suggested that we add a second kill criterion that, instead of resulting in a pivot, would result in us reviewing and narrowing our project's scope. ChatGPT suggested reviewing the kill criterion into something much stronger, and pivoting if that criterion was met. The two also had different feedback. ChatGPT made a 6-week outline for our project with where we should be wee by week, which Claude criticized as "plausible fiction." Overall, Claude was much less confident in the foundations of the project, while ChatGPT seemed to accept that foundation with little review. From this review, we decided to change our Evaluation section to include more basic goals first, and remove the less clear evaluations. We also made it clear that certain evaluations had to be done and recorded before others, which better outlined the order tasks had to be in the project.
